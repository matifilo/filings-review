Headline

Logitech International grew revenue to USD 1,218.8m in Q2 FY2027, up 2.8%, the slowest year-on-year growth since Q4 FY2025. Margins held up: gross margin was 44.9% and operating margin 16.5%, and net income rose 5.5% to USD 180.0m. Cash generation weakened, with free cash flow of USD 148.8m against USD 212.3m a year earlier.

Growth and profitability

Revenue growth of 2.8% compares with 6.9% in Q1 FY2027 and 6.3% in Q2 FY2026. In the last eight quarters, only Q4 FY2025 was weaker, at -0.1%. For 6M FY2027, revenue reached USD 2,446.1m, up 4.8%. The pack does not show why growth slowed.

Gross margin improved by +1.5 pp from 43.4% a year ago. It was well below the 49.5% reported in Q1 FY2027, a figure flagged for checking below.

Operating expenses grew as a share of revenue. Selling and marketing rose to 18.0% of revenue from 16.8%. R&D rose to 6.8% from 6.4%. General and admin was flat at 3.5%. With spending higher, operating margin rose by only +0.3 pp from 16.1%. That is below the 21.1% of Q1 FY2027 and the 20.1% of Q3 FY2026.

Net margin was 14.8%, against 14.4% a year earlier. For 6M FY2027, net income was USD 415.7m, up 31.3%, well ahead of the second-quarter growth rate.

Working capital and cash

Operating cash flow fell to USD 163.6m from USD 228.8m. Trailing-twelve-month cash conversion eased to 1.25x from 1.29x, so operating cash flow still comfortably exceeds net income.

Inventory rose 7.6% to USD 556.8m, growing faster than revenue. A year earlier, inventory had fallen 0.5%. Days inventory outstanding reached 75 days, the highest of the last eight quarters, up from 66 days in Q1 FY2027 and 70 days a year ago.

Days payables outstanding lengthened to 87 days from 79 days. As a result, the cash conversion cycle shortened to 44 days from 46 days despite the inventory build. Days sales outstanding were unchanged at 55 days.

Share buybacks of USD 170.8m exceeded free cash flow in the quarter and were well above the USD 106.6m of Q2 FY2026. Cash and cash equivalents stood at USD 1,507.6m, up from USD 1,375.8m a year earlier.

What to watch next quarter

  • Revenue momentum: whether growth recovers from 2.8% or slows further. The pack does not explain the slowdown.
  • Inventory: whether days inventory outstanding of 75 days comes down, or whether stock keeps growing faster than sales.
  • Supplier terms: the shorter cash conversion cycle relies on longer payables of 87 days, which may not last.
  • Operating spending: whether selling and marketing at 18.0% of revenue keeps absorbing the gross margin gains.
  • Capital returns: whether buybacks continue to exceed free cash flow.
  • Q1 FY2027 margins: whether the first-quarter figures are confirmed or revised.

Data caveats

  • Gross margin, Q1 FY2027: reported at 49.5%, well above the other recent quarters, such as 43.2% in Q3 FY2026 and 44.5% in Q4 FY2026. This is an unusual figure that needs checking against the filing. The pack gives no explanation.
  • Operating margin, Q1 FY2027: reported at 21.1%, a large jump from 14.1% in the same quarter last year. This is also an unusual figure that needs checking, and the pack gives no explanation.
  • Effect on the half-year: both flagged figures feed into the 6M FY2027 results, including net income growth of 31.3%.
  • Derived figures: Q4 figures and most quarterly cash flows are derived by subtracting the previous year-to-date figure from the current one. They may differ slightly from the company's own presentation.
  • Missing items: none are listed as missing in the pack.
