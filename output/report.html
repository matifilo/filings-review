Logitech InternationalLOGI · SEC filer · fiscal year ends March
Quarterly review · Q2 FY2027

Logitech International grew revenue to USD 1,218.8m1 in Q2 FY2027, up 2.8%2, the slowest year-on-year growth since Q4 FY2025.

Margins held up: gross margin was 44.9%3 and operating margin 16.5%4, and net income rose 5.5%5 to USD 180.0m6. Cash generation weakened, with free cash flow of USD 148.8m7 against USD 212.3m8 a year earlier.

Growth and profitability

Revenue growth of 2.8%9 compares with 6.9%10 in Q1 FY2027 and 6.3%11 in Q2 FY2026. In the last eight quarters, only Q4 FY2025 was weaker, at -0.1%12. For 6M FY2027, revenue reached USD 2,446.1m13, up 4.8%14. The pack does not show why growth slowed.

Gross margin improved by +1.5 pp15 from 43.4%16 a year ago. It was well below the 49.5%17 reported in Q1 FY2027, a figure flagged for checking below.

Operating expenses grew as a share of revenue. Selling and marketing rose to 18.0%18 of revenue from 16.8%19. R&D rose to 6.8%20 from 6.4%21. General and admin was flat at 3.5%22. With spending higher, operating margin rose by only +0.3 pp23 from 16.1%24. That is below the 21.1%25 of Q1 FY2027 and the 20.1%26 of Q3 FY2026.

Net margin was 14.8%27, against 14.4%28 a year earlier. For 6M FY2027, net income was USD 415.7m29, up 31.3%30, well ahead of the second-quarter growth rate.

Working capital and cash

Operating cash flow fell to USD 163.6m31 from USD 228.8m32. Trailing-twelve-month cash conversion eased to 1.25x33 from 1.29x34, so operating cash flow still comfortably exceeds net income.

Inventory rose 7.6%35 to USD 556.8m36, growing faster than revenue. A year earlier, inventory had fallen 0.5%37. Days inventory outstanding reached 75 days38, the highest of the last eight quarters, up from 66 days39 in Q1 FY2027 and 70 days40 a year ago.

Days payables outstanding lengthened to 87 days41 from 79 days42. As a result, the cash conversion cycle shortened to 44 days43 from 46 days44 despite the inventory build. Days sales outstanding were unchanged at 55 days45.

Share buybacks of USD 170.8m46 exceeded free cash flow in the quarter and were well above the USD 106.6m47 of Q2 FY2026. Cash and cash equivalents stood at USD 1,507.6m48, up from USD 1,375.8m49 a year earlier.
