## Headline

Logitech International grew Q2 FY2027 revenue to USD 1,218.8m [F:cur.revenue], up 2.8% [F:cur.revenue_yoy]. That is the slowest year-on-year growth since Q4 FY2025. Net income rose 5.5% [F:cur.net_income_yoy] to USD 180.0m [F:cur.net_income], but operating cash flow fell to USD 163.6m [F:cur.operating_cash_flow] from USD 228.8m [F:ly.operating_cash_flow] a year earlier.

## Growth and profitability

Revenue growth slowed from 6.9% [F:q1_fy2027.revenue_yoy] in Q1 FY2027 and from 6.3% [F:ly.revenue_yoy] in Q2 FY2026. For 6M FY2027, revenue was USD 2,446.1m [F:ytd.revenue], up 4.8% [F:ytd.revenue_yoy]. Net income for the same period was USD 415.7m [F:ytd.net_income], up 31.3% [F:ytd.net_income_yoy]. The year-to-date figures include the flagged Q1 FY2027 quarter described under Data caveats.

Gross margin was 44.9% [F:cur.gross_margin], against 43.4% [F:ly.gross_margin] a year earlier, a change of +1.5 pp [F:cur.gross_margin_change_yoy].

Operating expenses rose as a share of revenue:
- Selling and marketing was 18.0% [F:cur.selling_pct] of revenue, against 16.8% [F:ly.selling_pct].
- R&D was 6.8% [F:cur.rnd_pct], against 6.4% [F:ly.rnd_pct].
- General and admin held at 3.5% [F:cur.admin_pct], the same as 3.5% [F:ly.admin_pct] last year.

As a result, most of the gross margin gain did not reach the operating line. Operating margin was 16.5% [F:cur.operating_margin], against 16.1% [F:ly.operating_margin], a change of +0.3 pp [F:cur.operating_margin_change_yoy]. Net margin was 14.8% [F:cur.net_margin], against 14.4% [F:ly.net_margin]. The pack does not say what drove the higher gross margin or the heavier selling spend.

## Working capital and cash

Cash generation weakened in the quarter:
- Free cash flow was USD 148.8m [F:cur.free_cash_flow], against USD 212.3m [F:ly.free_cash_flow] a year earlier.
- Share buybacks rose to USD 170.8m [F:cur.buybacks] from USD 106.6m [F:ly.buybacks], so buybacks were larger than free cash flow this quarter.
- Cash and cash equivalents still ended the quarter at USD 1,507.6m [F:cur.cash], up from USD 1,375.8m [F:ly.cash].
- On a trailing basis, operating cash flow covered net income 1.25x [F:cur.cash_conversion_ttm], slightly below 1.29x [F:ly.cash_conversion_ttm].

Working capital moved in two directions:
- Days sales outstanding were unchanged at 55 days [F:cur.dso_days], against 55 days [F:ly.dso_days].
- Days inventory outstanding rose to 75 days [F:cur.dio_days] from 70 days [F:ly.dio_days]. This is the highest level in the quarterly series, which ran as low as 60 days [F:q3_fy2026.dio_days] in Q3 FY2026.
- Inventory was USD 556.8m [F:cur.inventory], up 7.6% [F:cur.inventory_yoy]. That is faster than revenue growth, and compares with a fall of 0.5% [F:ly.inventory_yoy] a year earlier.
- Days payables outstanding lengthened to 87 days [F:cur.dpo_days] from 79 days [F:ly.dpo_days]. This more than offset the inventory build.
- As a result, the cash conversion cycle shortened to 44 days [F:cur.cash_conversion_cycle_days] from 46 days [F:ly.cash_conversion_cycle_days].

## What to watch next quarter

- **Revenue growth:** whether growth recovers toward the 7.4% [F:q4_fy2026.revenue_yoy] seen in Q4 FY2026 or keeps slowing from 2.8% [F:cur.revenue_yoy]. The reason for the slowdown is not in the data.
- **Gross margin:** whether it holds near 44.9% [F:cur.gross_margin]. The drivers are not disclosed in the pack.
- **Selling and marketing:** whether spend stays at 18.0% [F:cur.selling_pct] of revenue, and whether it supports growth.
- **Inventory:** whether days inventory outstanding of 75 days [F:cur.dio_days] reflects a deliberate build or slower sell-through. The pack does not say.
- **Buybacks and cash:** whether operating cash flow recovers, and whether buybacks continue to exceed free cash flow. The extended 87 days [F:cur.dpo_days] of payables may not be sustainable as a source of cash.

## Data caveats

- **Q1 FY2027 gross margin:** this was 49.5% [F:q1_fy2027.gross_margin], far above the other recent quarters, for example 44.5% [F:q4_fy2026.gross_margin] in Q4 FY2026. It is an unusual figure that needs checking against the filing.
- **Q1 FY2027 operating margin:** this was 21.1% [F:q1_fy2027.operating_margin], against 14.1% [F:q1_fy2026.operating_margin] in Q1 FY2026. It is also an unusual move that needs checking. The pack gives no explanation for either figure.
- **Derived figures:** Q4 figures and most quarterly cash flows are derived by subtracting the previous year-to-date figure from the current one. Quarterly cash flow comparisons should therefore be read with care.
- **Working-capital days:** these use quarter-end balances over trailing-twelve-month flows.
- **Missing data:** no items are listed as missing for this period.
